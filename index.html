How it works

Pin it. Issue it. Close it.

Four steps. Every date recorded by pin.

  1. 01Open

    Pin it on the drawing

    Tap the spot on the plan. Add the room number. Photograph it, mark it up, say what's wrong.

    Opened 14 Oct, 10:42
  2. 02Notified

    Issue it to the contractor

    Issue a whole list in one go. pin. dates it, builds the A4 notice and writes the covering note.

    Notified 15 Oct, 09:10
  3. 03Fixed

    They put it right

    The contractor opens the link. They reply, add photos and mark it fixed. No app needed.

    Fixed 22 Oct, 16:05
  4. 04Closed

    You inspect and close

    Check the photos or the work. Close it. Not right? Send it back with a note.

    Closed 24 Oct, 11:30
